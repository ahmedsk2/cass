# Rate limits key an IPv6 client on its /64

- **Context.** Every rate limit keyed by the client address used the whole address. One IPv6 subscriber is routed a whole /64 and can take a fresh address in it for every request, so a client rotating inside its /64 passed every one of those caps (backlog, "Submissions").
- **Decision.** Decided by the owner on 2026-11-16, on the design the backlog row gave. `App\Support\ClientIp::limiterKey()` is the key: the address as it is for IPv4, the /64 for IPv6 in one spelling (`2001:db8::1` and `2001:db8::2` are both `2001:db8::/64`), and the IPv4 address for one written as IPv6 (`::ffff:203.0.113.5`). It keys the route limiters `conference-assets` (for a guest), `file-download`, `submission-status` and `invitation-accept`; the public forms (the submission and its penalty, registration, contact, and the invitation page's accept and sign-in); both panel-login buckets; and the status-link cap of [0014](0014-status-link-caps-on-the-public-form.md), whose variable and default stay. `ClientIp::from()` still returns the full address, for Turnstile's `remoteip` and for an Action handed an address, which keys it with `ClientIp::limiterKeyFor()`.
- **Consequence.** Clients that share a /64, such as one office or venue network, share one budget, as clients behind one IPv4 NAT address already do. These stay on the full address: the short-link scan count (`RecordShortLinkVisit`), which never refuses a visitor and is capped per link as well; and the limits the framework keys on `request()->ip()`, which are Livewire's upload throttle (`config/livewire.php`) and Filament's own password-reset, email-verification and profile pages.
- **To reverse.** Make `ClientIp::limiterKeyFor()` return its argument unchanged (`app/Support/ClientIp.php`), and invert the IPv6 cases in `tests/Unit/ClientIpTest.php` and the /64 tests in `RateLimitInventoryTest`, `StatusLinkCapTest`, `SubmissionFormTest`, `AcceptInvitationTest` and `SubmissionStatusPageTest`.
