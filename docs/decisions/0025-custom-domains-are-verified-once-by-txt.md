# Custom domains are verified once, by the TXT record

- **Context.** A proxied CNAME is flattened into A records by Cloudflare's authoritative servers, so a public resolver asked for the CNAME gets nothing. Requiring it would refuse exactly the setup the runbook recommends and pass only the unproxied one, which leaks the origin IP. Once a domain is verified, Traefik holds a certificate for it and the host is in the trusted-host list.
- **Decision.** Proposed by the session on 2026-09-12 (Plan 6, Task 1, decision 2, `e1be4f1`) and accepted by the owner with the merge of #82, which adds this file (the owner approves that pull request): verification checks the TXT record and not the CNAME, and nothing re-verifies a verified domain. Re-verification is the **Verify** button.
- **Consequence.** A domain can verify and still not resolve, and the organiser learns that from Traefik. If an organiser deletes the TXT record, the domain keeps working: a nightly re-check that could take a live conference offline because a DNS provider had a bad minute is a worse failure than a stale record (backlog, 2026-09-12).
- **To reverse.** A CNAME lookup in `App\Actions\Organizations\VerifyCustomDomain`, whose docblock says it does not check the CNAME (`app/Actions/Organizations/VerifyCustomDomain.php:22`), and a scheduled re-verification in `routes/console.php`, which has none (`VerifyCustomDomain.php:69`: nothing re-checks a record on a schedule). Invert the cases in `tests/Unit/VerifyCustomDomainTest.php` and `tests/Feature/Organizer/CustomDomainTest.php` that verify a domain on its TXT record alone.
